The Actorly two-card mark with the lowercase "actorly" wordmark — use in navs, footers and cards instead of copying the SVG.
```jsx
<Logo size={22} />
<Logo size={28} onDark />
<Logo size={32} wordmark={false} />
```
- `onDark` for ink sections. Never recolour the ember front card; never set "actorly" in capitals or another font.
