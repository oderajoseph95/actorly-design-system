Why "who" dependson Shopify.

Shopify tells apps that something changed, but usually not who changed it. Actorly matches each product change against Shopify's own activity for the same product at the same time. When the match is clear, you see a name and how sure Actorly is. When it isn't, you see unknown.

Your team doesn't need to do anything differently. Edits made the normal way in Shopify are matched the same as any other.
