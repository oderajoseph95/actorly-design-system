Questions merchants ask.

What does Actorly track?+

Products and variants (including prices), inventory, collections, discounts, shipping profiles, locations, markets, themes, store settings, orders, fulfillments and files. See the full list, including what it can't see.

Is it really free?+

Yes. Watch is free forever, with every tracked part of your store, unlimited changes, 3 months of history and one restore a month. Restore is $29 a month for history kept for as long as you stay on Restore, and unlimited restores.

Can it show changes from before I installed it?+

No, and no app can, because Shopify doesn't keep a field-by-field history. Actorly records everything from the moment you install.

Does it always know who made a change?+

No. For product changes, Actorly shows who likely made it when Shopify reports it, with how sure it is. For other changes, and whenever Shopify doesn't say, it shows unknown instead of guessing. Your team keeps editing in Shopify as usual.

What can it put back?+

Select fields, one at a time, such as a variant's price or a product's tags. Actorly checks nothing changed since, asks you to confirm, writes the old value, then re-reads your store to make sure it took. If a restore isn't safe, it changes nothing.

Will it slow down my store?+

No. Actorly adds nothing to your storefront or theme. It works in the background from Shopify's own change notifications.

Am I charged per change?+

Never. Busy stores pay the same as quiet ones. Plans differ on how long history is kept and how many restores you get.
