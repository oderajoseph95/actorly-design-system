Security and data · Actorly

Your store's history,kept carefully.

Actorly records what changed in your store. Here is what it keeps, where it runs and what happens to it when you leave.

Specimen text: not the published security page.

What Actorly keepsThe old and new value of each tracked field, when it changed, and who made it when Shopify reports it.
What it holds about customersActorly stores some personal details about customers when they appear in a change it records: a name, whether they agreed to marketing, a default address and the addresses on an order, including phone numbers, and the notes on a customer, an order or a draft order. It does not fetch payment details, card numbers or tracking numbers. When Shopify sends an erasure request for a customer, those details are erased; details on a draft order are erased when the store's data is erased.
Nothing on your storefrontNo scripts, no theme code, no app blocks. Shoppers never load anything from Actorly.
Writes only when you confirmActorly changes your store only during a restore you confirmed, one field at a time, and checks the result.

Where it runs.

HostingCloudflare Workers
StorageCloudflare, which encrypts it at rest
In transitTLS on every connection
Access to ShopifyShopify's own app permissions, approved by you at install and revoked when you uninstall

Who else handles data.

Three companies handle data for Actorly. The same list is in the data processing agreement.

Cloudflare, Inc.Hosting and storage of your change history.
PostHogCounts product events about Actorly itself, only when switched on. No customer data, no store content.
SlackOperational notices for the Actorly team: the store domain and a short status. No customer data, no store content.

Keeping and deleting.

Watch planHistory is kept for 3 months
Restore planHistory is kept for as long as you stay on Restore and keep Actorly installed
UninstallShopify sends a deletion request 48 hours later, then your history, saved evidence and account records are removed. Reinstalling within 48 hours keeps everything

Found a problem?

Email security@example.com with what you found and how to reproduce it. Please give us a reasonable time to fix it before sharing it publicly. We don't hold formal certifications such as SOC 2 today.
