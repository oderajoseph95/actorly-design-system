How to see who changeda product in Shopify.

What Shopify shows you, what it doesn't, and how to find who changed a price, title or status.

Example authorExample date7 min read
Short answerShopify shows the current value of a product, not its earlier ones, and it doesn't keep a field-by-field history. To see who changed something, you need a record that started before the change happened, and for product changes, a match against Shopify's own activity.

What Shopify keeps

Open a product in your Shopify admin and you see its values as they are right now. Save a new price and the old price is replaced. There's no built-in screen that lists every price a product has had, or the title it had last week.

Shopify does keep a record of some activity, and some apps can read it. But it isn't organised as "this field was X, then Y", and it doesn't cover every kind of change.

Why "who" is hard

When a product changes, Shopify can tell an app that it changed and what it changed to. It usually doesn't say who did it in the same message. Staff, apps, imports and bulk edits can all make the same kind of change.

The honest answer is sometimes "unknown". A tool that always shows a name is guessing some of the time.

How Actorly works it out

For each product change, Actorly looks at Shopify's own activity for the same product at the same time. If one person or app clearly lines up, you see their name with how sure Actorly is. If nothing lines up, you see unknown, and it stays that way.

What to do today

If a product changed recently and you don't know why, check with your team and the apps you've installed in the last few days. Then install a change history so the next time it happens, you have the answer: the old value, the new value, when, and who when Shopify reports it.
