actorly

Actorly is a Shopify app that keeps a searchable history of changes across the parts of your store listed on the coverage page, with the old value, the new value and, for product changes, who made it when Shopify reports it. Restore select fields, checked after every restore. Watch is free; Restore is $29 a month.
