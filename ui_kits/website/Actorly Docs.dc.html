Getting started.

Install Actorly and approve the permissions. There's nothing to set up and nothing is added to your theme.

1Actorly takes a first look. It reads the current value of the products, collections, inventory, discounts, shipping, markets, locations, themes and store settings it tracks, and of the pages, menus, files and other kinds it checks on a schedule. This is your starting point. Customers, orders, fulfillments, draft orders and selling plans are recorded only from their first change after you install.
2Changes start arriving. From now on, the changes Shopify reports appear in Changes. Some parts of the store are checked on a schedule instead.
3Keep working as usual. Your team edits in Shopify the normal way. Actorly records it in the background.

Changes from before you installed can't be recovered. Shopify doesn't keep them, so no app can show them.

Reading a change.

Open any row in Changes to see the detail: what changed, what it was before, what it is now, when it happened and who made it if Shopify reports it.

Who made it.

For product changes, Actorly matches the change against Shopify's own activity for that product at that time. You'll see how sure Actorly is, always with the name and the label side by side: Likely, Possible or Unknown.

Other parts of the store, like inventory or shipping, don't come with this information from Shopify, so they show "Unknown". See which parts show who.

Putting it back.

On fields that support it, a change shows a Restore button. A restore puts back one field at a time and runs in this order:

1Read the field's value in Shopify right now.
2Check it still matches what Actorly recorded. If someone changed it since, stop.
3Ask you to confirm the exact value that will be written.
4Write the old value to Shopify.
5Read the store again and check the value really took.
6Record the restore in your history, like any other change.

If a check before the write fails, nothing is changed. If the check after the write cannot confirm the value, Actorly says so and asks you to look at the field in Shopify. Restores work one field at a time on select fields, such as a product's title, status or tags, a variant's price or a collection's title. Each field's support is listed on the coverage page.

Exporting.

On the Restore plan, export your history as CSV or JSON from the Data room. Choose a date range, a type of record, a level of detail (Simple, Deep or Very detailed) and CSV or JSON.

Plans and history.

Watch is free: every tracked part of the store, unlimited changes, 3 months of history and one restore a month. Restore is $29 a month or $240 a year: unlimited history, unlimited restores and export. Change plans in your Shopify admin; billing is on your Shopify invoice.

If you uninstall, your storefront is untouched and your Actorly history is deleted after Shopify's standard waiting period.

Troubleshooting.

A change I made isn't showing+

Most changes appear soon after Shopify reports them. Parts checked on a schedule, like pages and menus, show up at the next check. If it's been longer, check the Monitoring page in the app for anything Actorly couldn't reach.

It says "unknown" for who made it+

Shopify didn't report enough to match the change to a person or app. Actorly won't guess, and it won't change unknown to a name later.

The Restore button is missing+

That field can't be restored yet, or you've used this month's restore on the free plan. The coverage page lists what can be put back.

A restore stopped part way+

The field changed again after Actorly recorded it, so writing the old value could overwrite someone's newer edit. Open the latest change for that field and restore from there.
