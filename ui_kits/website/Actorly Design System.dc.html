Each section has its own move. One pinned scroll scene per page (the hero), plus at most two more. Transform and opacity only. Loops pause off screen and stop when clicked. Reduced motion shows the finished state. Engine: motion-kit.js with Lenis.

Copy.

Say"Change history." "The old value and the new value." "Who made it, when Shopify reports it." "Put select fields back, verified." "Unknown" when Shopify doesn't say. Numbers over adjectives; example figures labelled "Example store".
Don't say"One-click restore". "Who made every change." "Store activity log" (Shopify's own feature). Anything about sales or revenue. Email or Slack alerts, bulk restore or anything not built yet. Never knock Shopify or a competitor.
Source of truthClaims come from the product's coverage table and the product team's facts pack, never from a design file. Quote counts from the coverage table, never a typed number. Who-made-it is claimed for product changes only; restores are named only for fields the product has proven end to end.
